Program Overview

Structured, child-friendly chess learning

CFK classes balance fundamentals with hands-on play. Every session combines short lessons, puzzles, and supervised games to keep children engaged while strengthening concentration and decision-making skills.

Weekly classes

Dedicated beginner and intermediate groups with progression milestones that match each child's pace.

Friendly competitions

Regular mini-tournaments, puzzle races, and team games help students practise tactics in a supportive setting.

Parent updates

Coaches share highlights, strengths, and focus areas so families can celebrate progress together.
